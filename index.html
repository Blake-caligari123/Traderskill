Questions

Good to know.

How long does one flip last?

Most of it happens in the first few minutes, as streams of sand build the main shapes. The fine grains keep drifting down for a while after that. Flip again whenever you like.

Does it need batteries or power?

No. It's just sand, liquid, glass and gravity.

The sand stopped falling. Is it broken?

Almost certainly not. Sand sometimes clings to the glass. Give the frame a gentle quarter-turn or a light tap and it'll start again. Small air bubbles are normal and help the sand move.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed in 1–3 business days. Delivery within Australia usually takes 7–14 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

What if it arrives damaged?

Email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement free of charge. You don't need to return the broken one.

What's your return policy?

You have 30 days from delivery to return it for a full refund. Get in touch and we'll walk you through it. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law always apply.

Is it safe around kids and pets?

It's made of glass, so put it on a stable surface out of reach of small children and pets, and supervise younger kids when they flip it.
